# Product Goal

MissionVault Sentinel transforms satellite telemetry anomalies into explainable incident investigations, evidence-backed recommendations, and human-reviewed operational decisions.

---

# Design Principle

AI assists the mission operator.

AI does not autonomously command the spacecraft.

The final operational decision remains with a human operator.

# Limitations

MissionVault Sentinel is an experimental prototype and does not provide certified flight software or autonomous spacecraft control.

Its telemetry is simulated and its AI analysis is intended for decision support rather than autonomous mission operation.
